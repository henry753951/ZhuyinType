ZhuyinType 基本詞庫：CC BY-SA 4.0

The bundled lexicon.tsv is an adaptation combining Traditional forms and
Taiwan-preferred readings from CC-CEDICT, Big5 readings from Unicode Unihan,
and frequency scores from wordfreq. ZhuyinType converts readings to Zhuyin,
filters entries and computes unigram log probabilities. No endorsement by
the original authors is implied. Changes and build code:
ml/src/zhuyintype_ml/data/lexicon.py in the ZhuyinType source
distribution. Source list: resources/SOURCES.md (also included as
Licenses/SOURCES.md).

Reading channel (channel.bin, 0.4.3 and later): which other readings a
character may be typed with, and how likely each is, so that 有邊讀邊, a
neutral tone typed with its full tone and Taiwan readings inside words still
find the word. channel.bin is compiled from tables ZhuyinType derives
(ml/src/zhuyintype_ml/training/lexicon/channel.py) from CC-CEDICT, English
Wiktionary via Wiktextract, CNS11643 and wordfreq (all credited above) and:

Unicode Unihan 18.0.0 (kHanyuPinyin, kXHC1983, kTGHZ2013, kMandarin readings;
kHanyuPinlu counts): Copyright Unicode, Inc. and contributors.
