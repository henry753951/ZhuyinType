ZhuyinType 基本詞庫：CC BY-SA 4.0

The bundled lexicon.tsv is an adaptation combining Traditional forms and
Taiwan-preferred readings from CC-CEDICT, Big5 readings from Unicode Unihan,
and frequency scores from wordfreq. ZhuyinType converts readings to Zhuyin,
filters entries and computes unigram log probabilities. No endorsement by
the original authors is implied. Changes and build code: data/tools/ in
the ZhuyinType source distribution. Source list: data/SOURCES.md (also
included as Licenses/SOURCES.md).
