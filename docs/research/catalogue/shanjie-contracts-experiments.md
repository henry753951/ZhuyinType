## How to read the numbers

- 善解 scores are **log10**, ours are natural log. A δ of 1.0 log10 is about 2.3 nat.
- **fixed/broken (改對/改壞)** = rows a change makes right / wrong against a paired baseline; **net** = fixed − broken; **p** = exact two-sided McNemar unless noted; **UB** = 95% Poisson upper bound of the break rate.
- **Profiles:** 善解 has two hard profiles, **chat** and **formal**, selected per app. They roughly correspond to our chat/wiki mixture weight c.
- **Eval sets:** dev302 (hand-written dev), typing76 (real typing), 錯字回報 / user-reported (the maintainer's error reports, one-sided), cvtune and wikitune (Common Voice/Tatoeba and Wikipedia tuning sets, readings generated by rule), lytune (Legislative Yuan speech, 4,811 rows), discordtune (the maintainer's private Discord chat, 4,958 rows), chatclause / chatjudge / chatjudge-2 (the maintainer's own messages, split into tuning and one-shot judge sets), holdout (227 rows, later 3 × 300-row shards).
- **Their stack** (v0.5.0): McBopomofo base lexicon plus overlays and packs → word bigram (model-v6, 9.1M) with Brown-class backoff, Kneser–Ney continuation side file, formal-only trigram (7.7M) → n-gram top-8 → 14.4M char LM "會意" SJ-SR-6-14M (6L/384, int8), with gated fusion (α, τ, δ) + colloquial term + reading-channel term. The core is Rust; the shell is Swift InputMethodKit.

## Executive summary

**Differences from ZhuyinType.** The two projects made different model choices. 善解's reranker is a 14.4M causal char LM with a context of at most 2 Han characters (64 since context-return). ZhuyinType instead uses a syllable-conditioned model (v3) inside the lattice, a key-level typo and timing decoder, and KeyChannel. 善解 tested bigger models, distillation, LLM judges and Bayesian learning with paired statistics and published the negative results, which are useful evidence for us too.

**What we learned from them.** 善解's **process and product engineering** is careful and well documented, and it is where we have the most to learn:

- **Statistics:** paired McNemar and bootstrap on every change, break-rate caps, holdout rotation, and a private real-chat judge set.
- **Gated fusion:** the reranker may change a sentence only when the n-gram margin is small (τ) and its own gain is large enough (δ).
- **Behaviour matched to Apple Zhuyin:** re-pick freeze, digits typed inside the composition, English runs, punctuation names.
- **Narrow, heavily tested personal learning:** mirror and pollution tests.
- **Data packs:** packs with collision gates, a names layer, and Taiwan-specific corpus conversion fixes.
- **macOS plumbing:** secure-input privacy gate, Caps Lock-safe installation, hardened Sparkle, notarized release workflow, CI with mutation tests, KPI table.

Most items marked "adopt" below are cheap engineering. The biggest model-side lessons are three:

- Gate the reranker by breaks, not by mean accuracy.
- Add colloquial evidence as a gated rerank feature relative to top-1.
- Train the LM on expected (forward-backward) counts, not on one hard segmentation.

## Glossary for the neural section, and the 0c port explained

- **M / "會意" `sj-sr-6-14m`**: their shipped reranker. Char-level causal decoder, 6 layers, d 384, 6 heads, 14.4M params, vocab 9,717 (9,714 Han + PAD/UNK/BOS), ctx 64, pre-LN, tied embedding, GELU-tanh. int8 `.sjtl` 14.6 MB. Context fed = `context_key` = **≤ 2 Han chars** (stops at any punctuation/non-Han). Trained 1 epoch, 255M tokens (94.3% Wikipedia, colloquial ×5).
- **Fusion `pick(ng, ll, α, τ, δ)`**: rerank only n-gram top-8; if `ng[0]-ng[1] ≥ τ` keep #1; else j = argmax(ng + α·ll); switch only if fused(j) − fused(0) ≥ δ (log10 units).
- **fixed / broken (改對 / 改壞)**: rows the change makes right / makes wrong vs a baseline; "net" = fixed − broken; "break rate" = broken ÷ n; "UB" = 95% exact-Poisson upper bound of broken ÷ n.
- Sets: **discordtune** (maintainer's real Discord chat, 4,958 rows, dev layer, reused many times), **chatclause** (maintainer's own messages, tuning 5,761 / judge 6,559), **chatjudge / chatjudge-2** (fresh one-shot private judge sets, 2,972 / 5,783), **cvtune / wikitune** (Common Voice+Tatoeba / Wikipedia tuning), **dev302**, **typing76**, **user-reported** (error-report set, one-sided), **lytune** (Legislative Yuan colloquial speech, 4,811), **wikitypo** (155), **wikijudge1-4** (fresh Wikipedia article ranges).
- **Ours**: v2b reranker = char causal Transformer 8L/512, 31M params, 12k vocab, ctx 64, top-16, `score = engine + 1.0·logP`, only a +4.0 "exact keys first" margin (`nlp/engine/Sources/ZhuyinML/NeuralReranker.swift`, no τ/δ gate, no learned-word guard). v3 = syllable→char model (6L/512 causal context encoder ≤255 chars + 6L/512 bidirectional syllable encoder), per-position log P(char | syllables, context) at weight 0.5 inside the lattice.

### "0c" explained (their port of Funmula's 0c model)

**What it is.** 0c (靈犀注音 0c, by BigGo / Funmula) is a macOS Zhuyin IME whose architecture is almost the same as Shanjie's (lexicon + Viterbi candidates → Transformer reranks top-8, ≤64 chars of context). Its reranker `Funmula/0c-sequence-d512` (HF revision `d512-pf512-20260926`, commit `66eb4a81…`, `model.onnx` 130,723,134 bytes, SHA `71af1f0e…`) is **MIT-licensed weights**; the IME app source (`github.com/0c-biggo/0c-ime`) is **AGPL-3.0** (Shanjie only read it, never copied code). Their public numbers: 6,000 Taiwanese news sentences with dictionary-synthesized readings, statistical engine top-1 76.9% → 86.0% with the Transformer; P95 ≈ 7–16 ms, falls back to lexicon order past 100 ms. Their own fusion: engine + 2 × model, switch when the gap > 0.

**Architecture (reverse-engineered from the ONNX graph, 404 nodes, `tools/convert_oc.py` header):**
- Inputs per sequence: `ids, bases, tones, types, positions`, causal mask, `proj_pos`, `target_node/target_ids`. Sequence layout: `[BOS] context[-64:] [SEP] reading×n [OUT] candidate[:-1]`; scores are taken at the n positions from `[OUT]` on (so the model sees the **whole reading of the clause before any output char** → it computes P(candidate | context, full reading)).
- Embedding: reading positions (`types==1`) use `base_embedding[base] + tone_embedding[tone]` (initial+final "base" and tone factored), other positions use the tied `decoder.weight[id]`; plus `type_embedding`; then LayerNorm (weight only, eps 1e-5).
- 8 layers, d 512, 8 heads × 64, q/k/v/o 512×512 no bias, **RoPE** (rotate-half), layer 0 has no pre-attention LN (layers 1–7 do). FFN: LN → 512×1536 → split → GELU(erf)(first half) × second half (GEGLU) → 768×512, residual.
- Head: final LN → gather at `proj_pos` → dense 512×512 (no bias) → GELU → LN → × tied `decoder.weight[out_ids]ᵀ` + bias `val_779[out_ids]` → LogSoftmax over 27,859 columns (`out_ids` = all ids except 0–3, 5, 7; char id c ↦ column c−6). 32,638,433 params; vocab 27,857 chars (vs M 9,714).
- Scoring rules (`experiments/x0c/score_0c.py`): last syllable additionally tried as neutral tone when its base is in `neutral_tolerant_last` (max kept); rows outside 2–32 syllables or with OOV chars are skipped (1–2% of rows); batch is fixed at 1 so the 8 candidates are packed as a prefix tree (diff vs one-by-one ≤ ln 3.1e-5).
- App behaviours worth knowing (read from AGPL source, re-described only): reranks **synchronously in the keystroke with a 100 ms deadline**; protects user picks by **per-candidate veto** (only candidates that would rewrite a user-chosen span are excluded) instead of skipping the whole segment; context = only the IME's own committed, verified text, ≤64 chars; a numbers-only, default-off flight recorder.

**Shanjie's port (`oc-experimental.md`, `core/src/oc.rs`, `tools/convert_oc.py`, `tools/oc_ref.py`):** ONNX → own container `SJOC0001` (120-byte header with source SHAs, vocab/bases/neutral list/out_ids, f32 tensors 4-byte aligned) → numpy reference (vs ORT max |Δlog10| 3.15e-5, 300/300 same argmax) → zero-dependency Rust forward (Accelerate `cblas_sgemm` on macOS, libm `erff`, weights read **in place from a read-only mmap** so pages count in RSS but not phys_footprint). int8 failed parity (296/300, p99 0.437 log10; best variant per-output-column + f32 embedding 300/300 but p99 0.109 and ~76 MB), so f32 130.7 MB mmap is used. Per-candidate forward measured p95 74.5 ms with 64-char context and +273 MiB RSS (both over gates 50 ms / 250 MiB); adding **prefix KV sharing** (context+reading+[OUT] computed once per row, each candidate runs only its own n−1 chars) gave p95 33.9 ms (64-char ctx) / 27.2 ms (no ctx), RSS peak +227.5 MiB (0c alone +146.2), Rust vs ORT 4.07e-5, 300/300; 7 + 7 mutation tests caught. Planned as a default-off "experimental switch" (slice B not done: needs XPC helper per their R8 rule for third-party/downloaded weights, `try_score` to disable on corruption).

**What it achieved on their data:** X0c (vs their tinylm-v3, chat, 0c params α 1.5 τ 1 δ 2): judge period +142 net, discordtune +127 (189/62), user-reported +11, but **wikitune −88 and lytune −56** (chat-leaning). P1 (current decoder): 0c vs shipped M on discordtune 165/60, net **+105**, p 1.6e-12; single-pick accuracy on fixable rows 66.5% vs M 45.1%, retention 91.6% vs 86.8%; its 64-char context contributed only ≈ +20 rows (47 changed picks). Five distillation attempts into M-sized students all failed (see rows 26–28). The maintainer chose not to ship 0c as the default ("we'd lose our research and can't claim it as ours"); it is used as a yardstick and teacher.

**Relevance to ZhuyinType:** 0c is the closest public analogue to our v3 (reading-conditioned, long context, 8L/512 ≈ v2b's size). It is external evidence that P(cand | context, full reading) at ~32M params scores well above a pure char LM on 善解's chat sets; 善解's own small reading-conditioned experiments at 14M scale did not reach that result (B29, B30). We use a related formulation (v3) but have not yet measured it on a shared test with 0c; its MIT-licensed weights make it a useful reference model and teacher.

## 13. Negative results and pitfalls reported by 善解 (consolidated)

### Data and lexicon
- **Hard segmentation in LM training leaves the split path with zero bigrams.** With 後到 in the lexicon, c(後,到)=0, so "十分鐘後道" won. No smoothing change can fix counts that never entered the table (A6).
- **Running s2t or TWPhrases over text that is already traditional corrupts it.** 后/里/干 become 裡長/鄰裡/皇後. 床 becomes 牀. A blanket TWVariants pass zeroes 排泄 (422→0). 插件 becomes 外掛. Every fix cycle needed a full rebuild (A7, A8).
- **Evaluating a conversion change on references made by one of the conversions** favours that conversion: wikitune −9 vs wikitune-mw +17. Judge only on rows that neither conversion changes (A11, A12).
- **Words with no LM evidence and a flat entry score lose almost always.** 2-char pack names lose 98.7%, 3-char 38.9%. Each loses to a chain of common characters (宿儺 → 素娜), not to a rival word (C10).
- **Marking every pack word as unknown to the LM** broke 22 rows and fixed 25. Only reviewed readings should bypass the LM (C11).
- **Capping lexicon priors by corpus frequency buries rare names.** Raising the unseen penalty to 2.0 nearly doubled 3-char name losses (A39).
- **Resegmenting the corpus with an enlarged vocabulary steals counts from common pairs** (萊+恩 1,336→0) and caused a significant wikitune veto (A40).
- **Rebuilding a pack with a newer decoder silently dropped shipped names.** Separate the word list from the scores, and recheck shipped words against the previous pack (C9, C13).
- **Bulk-demoting "non-MOE" readings** would hit 5,817 entries that are mostly legitimate sandhi or neutral-tone readings (C25).
- **Rule-labelled reading counts** had a 40.8% label error rate and judge5 net −1,457 (A32).
- **Penalising particles typed with tone 1** cost lytune −213 to −508, because users type 啦 as ㄌㄚ (A31).
- **Softmax-normalised reading probabilities** charge log10(k) to every word with k tied readings (A29).

### Models and fusion
- **An ungated reranker breaks too many rows.** Without δ, the shipped α/τ setting broke 142 rows on the judge period; with δ 0.25 it broke 16 (B2).
- **A break-rate cap met on the tuning set does not transfer.** Cells at 0.46–0.50% on tuning landed at 0.54–1.72% on fresh data. Select with a tighter cap (0.3%) and judge on the Poisson UB (B12, B17, B24).
- **"1 break = 10 fixes" scoring** threw away real gains (dev302 276→249). 1:1 net plus a break cap worked better (B11).
- **Bigger models on the same data did not help chat.** L 30M (our v2b's shape) lost to M 14M by −97 on real chat. XL helped only Wikipedia (tokens per parameter fell from 17 to 4) (B3, B18).
- **Five ways of distilling a strong reranker (0c) into a causal char LM all failed**: candidate KL, plus readings, plus capacity, position-level KL, and reweighting (B25–B27).
- **A listwise objective with n-gram gap and rank as features** collapsed to "keep #1" (pick-right 19%) (B28).
- **Feeding readings to a causal LM position by position** computes the wrong quantity, Σ log P(c_i | c_<i, R_≤i), and lost 71 rows. Readings must be given up front, as our v3 does (B29).
- **Mismatched context length between training and evaluation** (long in training, short in evaluation) lost 39 rows (B31).
- **Colloquial evidence applied before fusion "steals the anchor"**: 64 breaks such as 一戰→一站 and 致病→治病. Anchor on top-1 and gate the term by the neural model (B45, B46).
- **LLM list-choice judges are position-biased.** Apple FM picked #1 72% of the time, and order reversal flips 36–54% of picks. Author-written dev sets pointed the wrong way (dev302 +27 vs real chat −81) (B36, B40).
- **Crediting a new corpus without a reseeded same-data control** is unsafe: retraining alone moved net by 32 rows (B52).

### Learning
- **Context-free learning boosts pollute common words.** One 再 pick in two contexts regressed 24 of 54 ㄗㄞˋ rows (現在在做 → 現再再作), so the global layer was switched off (DL4).
- **Single-char learning under weak context** produced 我試買新的 and 你再. Shanjie now learns single chars only under an exact 2-Han context, and sentence-start keys carry the next syllable (DL5, DL6).
- **Learning from every commit learns fragments**: 80 of 317 correct sentences learned junk (DL1, DL14).
- **A Bayesian posterior with the LM as prior learns too little from one pick**: the mirror test passed 0–3/8, against 7/8 for a fixed boost (DL8).
- **Smoothing or trigram changes silently shrink personal boosts.** Under KN the mirror test fell from 7/8 to 4/8 (A22).
- **Rewriting the whole file with F_FULLFSYNC on every Enter** cost p95 16–20 ms (DL10).

### Product, shell and release
- **Re-registering or re-enabling the input source after a bundle swap breaks Caps Lock switching.** Enabling an IME the system has not accepted returns noErr and does nothing (E27).
- **Over-eager freezing** (re-freezing after a merge) regressed continued typing. Measure Apple first, then match it exactly (DE1).
- **Irreversible key decisions without a conservative bias**: 比賽用嗎1咬計時, and a displayed ㄅ committed as "1". Commit exactly what is displayed (DD3, DD4).
- **Successor-only prediction** lost about 20 points at "first syllable done". Score bonuses for learned predictions do not cross tiers, so reorder instead (DP2, DP7).
- **The abbreviation composer** shipped and was then hidden: keys got stuck, and 13–16% of words could not be reached (DP9).
- **Re-decoding the whole composition on every key** costs about 0.28 ms per syllable. Freeze the head beyond the reranker window (DE4).
- **Sparkle pitfalls:** `startingUpdater:true` can show a modal alert that hangs the IME. `SUFeedURL` is read from user defaults first. Skip-version keys can freeze updates. launchd remembers "no XPC at this path" until reboot (E36, E37, E39).
- **Exit codes that lie:** notarytool exits 0 on a rejected submission; actool exits 0 on an icon-name mismatch. The signing job's umask 077 made `/Library` copies unreadable (E41).
- **Windows of an LSUIElement app open behind the front app**, and settings taller than the screen end up under the Dock (E12, E13).
- **`NSLog`/`print` bypass the unified log when stderr exists**, so a log-privacy test must also capture fd 1 and 2 (E23).
- **Testing against Apple Zhuyin without restoring its dynamic lexicon** inflates Apple's score: 847 of 2,647 learned phrases came from the test itself (E55).
- **Running a shipping-ID sandboxed build locally** migrates real learning data into the container irreversibly (E25).
- **Self-imposed absolute gates** (digits "≥90%", missed by 3 rows) block good designs. Prefer paired, relative gates (DD1).
